Summary

Reading the chart

τ̂ is the softmax temperature fitted to each student's choices (payoff points, log scale): near 0 means always testing the current leader; large values mean choices barely depended on the sample means. τb is the share of non-greedy tests; τw weights each non-greedy test by how far below the leader it was, in units of the round's noise γ (a gap of γ or more counts fully).

The simulated benchmarks in the summary come from softmax agents with a fixed τ playing the same game in this page (same hidden-mean rule, starting trap, noise ±γ and test budget as the round in view; 1,500 games per point). Students often beat them at the same τ̂ because they explore early and exploit late, which a fixed-τ agent cannot do.

Total payoff chart. Realized payoff is the sum of the payoffs a student's tests actually returned (what they experienced, noise included). Expected payoff replaces each test by the true mean of the technology tested, so it isolates the cost of the student's choices from luck; its distance to the dotted line is the expected shortfall (regret). In a maximize-payoff round this is the score.

Hollow dots are students whose τ̂ sits at a grid bound: pure greedy play (left) or choices with no attraction to the leader (right). Only a bound on τ is identified for them.
